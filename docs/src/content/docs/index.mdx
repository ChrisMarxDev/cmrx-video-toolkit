## A toolkit for the whole workflow

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Edit with the transcript" icon="pencil">Review words, tighten cuts, attach animation notes and export a precise handoff.</Card>
  <Card title="Compose with code" icon="seti:video">Build scenes from Flutter components, deterministic motion, shaders and media.</Card>

## Review is part of the process

This is an early local-first toolkit. It is not a hosted service or an autonomous
editor. Preserve your original recordings, review the rough cut before animation,
and inspect the final output. Your media stays outside Git.
