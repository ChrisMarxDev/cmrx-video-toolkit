## Before you begin

This is an early local-first toolkit. Preserve original recordings, review the
rough cut before detailed animation and inspect the final output. Keep recordings,
transcripts and generated media outside Git.

The reusable components take your own colors and typography. The documentation’s
visual style does not determine how your videos look.
