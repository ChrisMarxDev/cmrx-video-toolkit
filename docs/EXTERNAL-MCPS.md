# Awesome Video Editing

A curated list of websites, tools and MCP servers for making videos.

## Contents

- [Visual effects](#visual-effects)
- [Music & sound effects](#music--sound-effects)
- [Memes](#memes)

## Visual effects

- [ASCII Magic](https://www.ascii-magic.com/) - ASCII, dithering, pixel art and glitch effects for images and videos. [MCP](https://www.ascii-magic.com/mcp-server).

## Music & sound effects

- [Soundza](https://soundza.com/) - Music, ambience and sound effects, plus sound generation. [MCP](https://soundza.com/developers).
- [SFXMint](https://sfxmint.com/) - CC0 sound effects and custom sound generation. [MCP](https://github.com/flreey/sfxmint-mcp).
- [Tunetank](https://tunetank.com/) - Music by mood and genre, plus sound effects. [MCP](https://github.com/tunetank/tunetank-mcp).

## Memes

- [Memelord](https://www.memelord.com/) - Generate and edit image memes and captioned video memes. [MCP](https://www.memelord.com/hackers/mcp).
