# Starter style questionnaire

Send these as one batch, adapting to known context. Do not send as ten successive
turns or require a form submission. A completed questionnaire is not approval of
the resulting style; preserve the skill's proposal/review distinction.

Suggested introduction:

“Every question is optional. Answer whichever you like, in bullets or your own
words; ‘you decide’ is fine too. More detail helps me tailor the style to you and
reduces guesswork. For anything you skip, I’ll suggest a sensible starting point.”

1. **What are you making, and for whom?** For example: tutorials, product demos,
   explainers, interviews or social clips. What should viewers understand, feel or do?
2. **Where will the videos appear?** Platforms, portrait/landscape/square, typical
   length and spoken/caption languages—whatever you already know.
3. **What brand should they fit?** Share a company/personal website, brand guide,
   existing channel or assets if available. What should carry over or change?
4. **What examples do you like or dislike?** Video/image links and timestamps are
   welcome. Tell me which traits matter: pacing, colors, framing, graphics or sound.
5. **How should it feel?** A few words such as calm, playful, technical, cinematic,
   minimal or energetic. Any strong dislikes or things we should avoid?
6. **What will be on screen?** You speaking, screen recordings, product footage,
   interviews, animation or a mix? Any framing/background preferences?
7. **What visual elements do you want?** Colors, fonts, light/dark themes, diagrams,
   charts, code, callouts, logos or recurring layouts. Existing components can be reused.
8. **How should editing and motion behave?** Relaxed or brisk cuts, subtle or bold
   transitions, restrained or expressive effects? Any effects to include or avoid?
9. **What about captions and audio?** Caption appearance/placement/highlighting,
   music mood or no music, sound effects and narration preferences.
10. **What else should shape the result?** Accessibility needs, required elements,
    production constraints, available assets, or anything you want me to decide.

Do not demand exact color codes, component IDs or technical effect parameters.
Translate answers and inspected references into proposed concrete values/recipes.
Save answers in the local style's interview.md, evidence in references.md and
unanswered preferences as proposals/open questions. No invented answers or approval.
